Backgammon Mastermind is a polished take on the classic strategy board game — with an interactive Learn to Play path that teaches real rules on a live board.

New to backgammon? Work through five short guided lessons covering the board, movement, hitting, the bar, and bearing off. Pass a quick check, then play your first real game.

Already know the game? Jump straight into a match against the computer or share the board with a friend in pass-and-play mode. Roll dice, move your checkers, hit blots, and bear off all fifteen to win.

Features:
• Learn to Play — interactive lessons on a real board
• Play against the computer
• Local 2-player pass-and-play
• Drag or tap to move; legal destinations highlighted
• Direction guide and beginner-friendly preferences
• Available in 17 languages
• No account and no ads — gameplay runs on your device
