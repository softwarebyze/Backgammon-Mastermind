# Privacy Policy

**Backgammon Mastermind** — last updated May 2026

## Summary

Backgammon Mastermind does not collect, store, or transmit personal data. All gameplay runs locally on your device.

## Data we collect

We do not operate a backend and do not collect account information, analytics tied to your identity, or gameplay telemetry.

## Local storage

The app may store preferences on your device (for example theme and language) using local storage (MMKV). This data never leaves your device unless you back up your phone through your OS provider.

## Third parties

The app does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in the current release.

## Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes

We may update this policy before store releases. The latest version lives in this repository.
